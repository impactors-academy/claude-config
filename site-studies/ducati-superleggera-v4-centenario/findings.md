# Site Study: Ducati Superleggera V4 Centenario
**URL:** https://superleggerav4centenario.ducati.com/fr/fr  
**Studied:** 2026-07-18  
**Slug:** `ducati-superleggera-v4-centenario`

---

## 1. Navigation & First Impression

**Screenshot:** `screenshots/01-hero-desktop.png`

The first viewport is an experience gate — a pure black stage with a draped fabric covering a motorcycle silhouette, lit dramatically from above in a single overhead pool of light. It reads as a theatrical product reveal before the product is ever shown. The eye goes immediately to the fabric shape at center, then to the centered heading above it, then to the single pill-shaped CTA at the bottom.

**Nav pattern:** Transparent floating bar with no background fill. It persists this way across all scroll stages — never picks up a fill or blur. Layout:
- Left: two text links ("REQUEST IT NOW" as a subtle CTA, "DUCATI.COM" as an external link)
- Center: Ducati shield logo
- Right: "CARACTÉRISTIQUES TECHNIQUES" (jump to specs) + "LANGUE" (language selector)

The nav disappears entirely from the intro gate — only the language selector is visible top-right during the pre-experience screen. This makes the first impression feel less like a website and more like a cinema presentation.

## 2. Hero Section

**Screenshots:** `screenshots/01-hero-desktop.png`, `screenshots/02-hero-post-intro.png`

The site enforces a mandatory entry ritual before revealing the bike:
1. Static frame with fabric-draped motorcycle shape + "COMMENCER L'EXPÉRIENCE" pill button
2. Click starts a video (blob URL, not a static source — streamed/generated). "Mute" and "Passer" controls appear once video starts.
3. Skipping or completing the video resolves to the true hero: the full motorcycle, red/white livery, in a dramatic 3/4 angle against black, with large heading + three stat callouts (power, weight, power-to-weight ratio).

**3D element check:** One canvas element is present — `class="home-page-scrolly-telling__canvas"`, rendered at 1920×1080, styled to fill its container. `window.THREE`, `window.BABYLON`, `window.spline` are all undefined — the engine is bundled into Next.js Turbopack chunks and not globally exposed. This is most likely a custom WebGL implementation or canvas-2D sprite-sheet animation, not a named engine. The canvas is used for the scrollytelling section (see §3), not the hero.

The hero entrance (post-intro) shows the full bike appearing against black — likely a quick cross-dissolve from the video end frame. No particle or wipe transitions visible at this point.

## 3. Scroll Behavior

**Screenshots:** `screenshots/03-scroll-stage-1.png` through `screenshots/03-scroll-stage-5.png`

**Total scroll height:** 20,104px (≈22× viewport height at 900px). This is an extremely long, content-dense page.

**Scroll mechanism:** Native window scroll. No Lenis/Locomotive Scroll wrappers detected. During the intro, `body[data-scroll-locked="1"]` (from `react-remove-scroll`) blocks all scrolling until the video completes or is skipped.

**Scroll-linked content — the canvas scrollytelling section:**

This is the defining UX pattern of the page. A single canvas element renders different imagery as scroll position advances. Observed stages:

- **Stage 1** (`03-scroll-stage-1.png`): Extreme close-up of the bike's nose fairing — carbon fibre texture fills ~60% of the viewport. Right-aligned text callout appears at the edge.
- **Stage 2** (`03-scroll-stage-2.png`): Low-angle shot of the front wheel and carbon-ceramic brake disc, almost photographic. Left-aligned heading. The transition between stages is not a page scroll but appears to be a canvas-redraw keyed to scroll position — the image changes as you scroll, not as you click.
- **Stage 3** (`03-scroll-stage-3.png`): Full bike in motion with speed-blur aerodynamic lines rendered around it. Dynamic composition, heading left-aligned.
- **Stage 4** (`03-scroll-stage-4.png`): The standout frame — full-viewport wireframe/X-ray rendering of the motorcycle, bright white technical drawing lines on black, with the actual engine rendered in photorealistic detail at center. This is entirely canvas-rendered. No static image serves this — it is generated by the scrollytelling system.
- **Stage 5** (`03-scroll-stage-5.png`): End of scrollytelling section, back to a photographic full-bike shot. Chapter navigation tabs appear below ("INGÉNIERIE EXTRÊME", "UN NOUVEAU MOTEUR", "UN RÊVE DE COLLECTION", "EXPÉRIENCE TRICOLORE"). Footer below.

**Video transitions between sections:** Five MP4 clips are preloaded from `assetv24b.com` CDN (`dynamics-dsk.mp4`, `engine-opt.mp4`, `tricolore-head-opt.mp4`, `tricolore-side-dsk-forward.mp4`). These play as transition wipes between the scrollytelling canvas stages — serving as cinematic cuts rather than scroll-linked animations.

## 4. Micro-Interactions

**Custom cursor:** None. No `[class*="cursor"]` elements found. The site uses the standard system cursor throughout.

**Hover states:** Not directly testable via Playwright screenshot, but the DOM reveals chapter tabs (accordion/tab pattern at the footer of the scroll section) and the nav CTAs. The "REQUEST IT NOW" link resolves to a Ducati contact form subdomain — it behaves as a standard text-underline link.

**Scroll prompt:** A "Faites défiler" (scroll) text button appears at the bottom of the intro hero section, as a gentle nudge to begin the canvas scroll sequence.

**Language selector:** A floating language selector appears as an overlay (`[data-state="open"]`) on initial load — it is the first thing to clear before the canvas section begins. It uses an `active` state on the button to indicate the dropdown is open.

## 5. Typography & Color

**Type system:**

The site uses a family of three fully custom, self-hosted Ducati brand fonts — no Google Fonts, no system serif fallbacks:

| Token | Font | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `ducatiStyleExt` | Extended/display weight | Primary headings (h2) |
| `ducatiStyleFont` | Regular | Body copy, UI labels |
| `ducatiStyleCondFont` | Condensed | Stats, technical callouts, secondary labels |

- **H2 (intro heading):** 32px / 700 / 1.2 line-height — appears large only by virtue of being centered in an otherwise empty full-viewport stage
- **Body/paragraph:** 18px / regular weight
- A fourth legacy token (`ducati-condensed, ducati, Arial, sans-serif`) appears in older utility styles

**Color palette:**

| Role | Value |
|---|---|
| Background | `#000000` — absolute black, no gradient |
| Primary text | `#ffffff` — pure white |
| Accent / brand red | `#cc0000` — CSS var `--color-primary: #c00` |
| Nav background | `rgba(0,0,0,0)` — fully transparent, always |

The tricolore livery (red/white with Italian flag stripe) is the only color relief in a screen otherwise locked to black-white-red.

## 7. Mobile Pass

**Screenshots:** `screenshots/07-mobile-hero.png`

At 390×844 (iPhone):

**Nav collapses to three elements:**
- Left: hamburger icon (`button "Menu Burger mobile"`)
- Center: Ducati logo
- Right: "LANGUE" text button

All desktop nav links ("REQUEST IT NOW", "DUCATI.COM", "CARACTÉRISTIQUES TECHNIQUES") are hidden behind the hamburger.

**Hero:** The draped-reveal poster switches to a different composition — `V24b reveal mobile poster` vs `V24b reveal desktop poster`. The mobile version frames the fabric-covered bike horizontally to fill the narrower viewport correctly. The CTA pill ("COMMENCER L'EXPÉRIENCE") anchors to the bottom of the screen, large and thumb-reachable. The heading text reflows to left-aligned, three short lines.

**Removed for mobile:** All horizontal text-image split layouts. The desktop scrollytelling canvas section is present in the DOM (`home-page-scrolly-telling__content-container`) but serves mobile-appropriate video sources (e.g., `tricolore-side-dsk-forward.mp4` has no explicit mobile alternate in the preloaded set — unclear if the canvas section degrades to static images on mobile).

**Overall:** The mobile version is a faithful simplification of the desktop — same black/white/red palette, same gated entry ritual, same canvas scrollytelling architecture — with the hamburger pattern managing the nav complexity.

## Summary — Techniques Worth Noting

1. **Gated experience ritual:** The mandatory video intro before any content is accessible is a bold trust-ask. It works here because the brand prestige and product exclusivity justify the friction. The "Passer" (skip) control is there for the impatient, but it's deliberately subordinate to the "Commencer" CTA.

2. **Full-viewport canvas scrollytelling:** The 20,104px page is structured around a single canvas element that re-renders full-bleed imagery keyed to scroll position. This creates the feel of flipping through a cinematic storyboard — each "section" is a different camera angle or technical visualization, not a layout component.

3. **X-ray technical reveal:** The wireframe/cutaway engine frame (stage 4) is the visual high point — a full-viewport technical illustration rendered inside the canvas, showing the bike as a ghost wireframe with the engine materializing in photographic detail. Extremely high craft signal for a technical product.

4. **Asset separation:** Transition videos served from a dedicated CDN (`assetv24b.com`), keeping the Next.js bundle lean while enabling high-quality video wipes.

5. **Absolute chromatic restraint:** Black + white + one brand red. No gradients, no photography backgrounds, no ambient color. Every piece of color is either the product itself or the Ducati shield. The bike is the only color event on the page.
