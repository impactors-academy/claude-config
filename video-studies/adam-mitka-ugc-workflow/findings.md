## 1. Overview

A creator-economy "how I make money" video pitching UGC content creation as
an income stream, structured as a teaching video with a soft funnel at the
end (DM for a paid program). Frames: `04-who-am-i-board.jpg`,
`01-fastest-way-hook-slide.jpg`.

## 2. Structure & pacing

Four-beat structure, each beat marked by a new whiteboard panel appearing
next to the talking head (a Miro-style live-annotated board, not slides):

1. **Hook + credibility** (0:00–1:08) — what the video promises, then a
   quick personal-proof section (background, current numbers) before any
   teaching starts.
2. **Concept explainer** (1:08–3:09) — what the offer/service actually is,
   framed against the more expensive alternative it's disrupting (paid
   influencer/agency ads vs. this cheaper regular-person alternative).
3. **Step-by-step guide** (3:09–8:35) — the actual workflow, broken into
   three labeled stages shown as connected nodes on the board: **Finding →
   Pitching → Stacking**. See `02-organic-ugc-stepbystep-board.jpg`.
4. **CTA + qualifier** (8:35–end) — a soft-close DM funnel, paired with a
   short "who this is NOT for" qualifier before the ask (see Takeaways).

Pacing is fast — new visual every 10–20s in the early sections, slowing
into longer static holds once the board fills up with the "how" content.

## 4. Technique / pattern notes — the actual workflow

This is the part worth adapting. Stripped of their specific niche, the
three-stage engine is a general outreach-and-delivery pattern:

**Finding**
- Build a portfolio *before* having real track record — fill gaps with
  self-initiated demo/mock pieces that look like real work, not a resume
  of intentions.
- Source leads by studying the target platform's own feed/community for
  who's already active in the category (organic discovery), not cold
  lists only — supplement with directory/marketplace sources for volume.

**Pitching**
- Outreach message shape: personalized opener referencing something
  specific about the target (not a generic template swap) → give away a
  small piece of tailored value up front, unprompted → link the
  portfolio → close with a low-effort yes/no ask, not an open-ended one.
- Two message variants shown, both short — outreach here is optimized for
  reply rate over information density.

**Stacking**
- Once one relationship is proven, run several in parallel rather than
  going deeper on one at a time — income (or throughput, in a non-revenue
  context) compounds by concurrency, and each new relationship also
  improves the pitch/portfolio for the next one.

**Presentation technique, separately from the workflow itself:** the
live-annotated whiteboard-next-to-camera format keeps a persistent visual
"map" of where in the framework the viewer currently is — worth noting as
a technique for any of our own explainer content, independent of the UGC
subject matter.

## 5. Takeaways

- The **Finding → Pitching → Stacking** shape is a clean, portable
  mental model for any outreach-based acquisition motion, not specific to
  UGC — could map directly onto how we structure partner/creator
  outreach or client acquisition playbooks.
- The "free value before the ask" pitch structure (research → give →
  link → low-friction close) is the most concretely reusable piece —
  worth turning into our own outreach template.
- The "no track record → make your own demo pieces" fallback for Finding
  is a good unblock pattern for anyone on our side without an existing
  portfolio in a given area.
- Presentation-wise, the persistent whiteboard-map format is worth
  testing for our own tutorial/explainer content — it's a device for
  showing progress through a framework, not tied to this topic.
- Standard caveat: the CTA and "who this is/isn't for" framing are sales
  mechanics on a course upsell — useful to recognize as a pattern, not
  something to adopt uncritically for a real acquisition workflow.
